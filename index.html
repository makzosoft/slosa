About SLOSA

St. Luke's Grammar School has, since its inception, been a citadel of excellence in academics, sports, and character formation. The school maintained this enviable status for decades until it was restructured to St. Luke's Grammar School (Junior) in line with Lagos State Government policy.

The products of this great institution have continued to make indelible marks in Nigeria and across the world. United under the banner of the St. Luke's Grammar School Old Students' Association, SLOSA, we are proud to be recognised as one of the most active and impactful alumni associations in Nigeria.

SLOSA has been in existence for over 15 years and is a duly incorporated association with the Corporate Affairs Commission, Nigeria. We operate a functional constitution that guides our affairs. The Association is governed by a National Executive Committee led by a President, with members democratically elected at our Annual General Meeting held every July, ahead of Founders' Day celebrations. In line with our constitution, no member may serve in the same office for more than two terms.

For several years, inadequate government funding and attention placed a strain on St. Luke's Grammar School. In response, SLOSA took up the challenge of restoring, preserving, and developing the physical, intellectual, moral, and social fabric of our alma mater. Since the rescue mission initiated by the administration of former Governor Akinwunmi Ambode, we have sustained the momentum by consistently mobilising resources. In a short period, our members have raised and prudently deployed colossal sums toward infrastructural and academic projects in the school, and the impact is clearly visible today.

Our commitment remains ongoing. Through donations and contributions from alumni and friends of the school both in Nigeria and in the diaspora, we continue to support the sustenance and growth of St. Luke's Grammar School (Junior). If you are a proud alumnus of St. Luke's Grammar School, please register with us. Visitors and friends of the school are also welcome to browse our activities and join our community. Together, we build for the future.

1980

St. Luke's Grammar School opens its doors in Bariga, Lagos State, adopting the motto For God and Humanity.

Restructuring

In line with Lagos State Government policy, the school becomes St. Luke's Grammar School (Junior).

The rescue mission

Under the administration of former Governor Akinwunmi Ambode, old students answer the call to restore the school.

Today

SLOSA continues to raise funds and deliver projects, with a National Executive Committee elected every year at the AGM.

Mission

To foster unity, service, and excellence among St. Luke's Old Students by promoting the welfare of members, supporting the growth and development of our alma mater, and upholding the values of integrity, faith, and community impact.

Vision

To be a leading, impactful alumni body that produces distinguished leaders, nurtures lifelong brotherhood and sisterhood, and transforms St. Luke's Grammar School and society through service, mentorship, and legacy projects.

Core Values

Integrity

We uphold honesty, transparency, and accountability in all we do.

Unity

We are one family, bound by the St. Luke's spirit of brotherhood and sisterhood.

Excellence

We strive for the highest standard in service, leadership, and character.

Service

We are committed to giving back to our alma mater, members, and community.

Faith

We are rooted in Godly values and moral discipline as taught at St. Luke's.

Legacy

We build and preserve institutions and projects that outlive us.
